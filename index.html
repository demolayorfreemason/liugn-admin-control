Access & Roles

Who runs Liugn — 𐌻𐌹𐌿𐌲𐌽, and exactly what each role is allowed to do. The owner sits at the top; every other role is partial and restricted.

Owner — Creator of Liugn — 𐌻𐌹𐌿𐌲𐌽
Full Control

The game's owner and the only holder of the owner key. This Admin Control room, the websites it governs, and everything published under the Sanctum name belong to the owner. All artwork, designs, names, and content here are the owner's own original work.

THE OWNER CAN
  • Open this room with the owner key
  • Set every site online, maintenance, or offline
  • Run Global Controls and the master maintenance switch
  • Set currency, starting coins, and daily rewards
  • Post announcements to any or all sites
  • Moderate players and keep the watchlist
  • Read the full activity log
  • Grant and revoke partial administrator access
OWNERSHIP
  • Owns Liugn — 𐌻𐌹𐌿𐌲𐌽 and the Sanctum brand
  • Owns the Game Site, Clothes Studio, and Cars Studio
  • Final say on every site, building, and account
Partial Administrator — Buildings & Real Estate
Restricted · Buildings Only

Trusted people the owner brings in to help grow the world — for example, real estate contributors who add houses and buildings to the game. Their access is partial by design: buildings, and nothing else.

THEY CAN
  • Add new buildings and houses to the game world
  • Work on buildings — place, edit, and update them
  • Work only in the building tools they are given
THEY CANNOT
  • Enter this Admin Control room or use the owner key
  • Change any site's status or the global switches
  • Touch currency, coins, rewards, or player accounts
  • Post announcements
  • Moderate, watch, or ban players
  • See the activity log or grant access to anyone

Partial administrator seats are assigned by the owner only, and the owner can revoke them at any time. No other roles exist on this site.
